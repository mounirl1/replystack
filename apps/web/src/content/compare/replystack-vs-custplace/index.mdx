
# ReplyStack vs Custplace: Complete Comparison for Review Management in 2026

Choosing the right review management platform impacts how effectively you handle customer feedback. Custplace has established itself as a comprehensive French platform for enterprises, while ReplyStack offers an innovative browser extension approach. This detailed comparison helps you find the best solution for your business needs.

## Overview of Both Solutions

### Custplace: The French Enterprise Platform

Custplace is a French SaaS platform specializing in comprehensive review management and reputation enhancement. Serving over 5,000 brands, Custplace is NF-certified for transparent review collection and management, making it particularly popular among French enterprises and multi-location businesses.

The platform focuses on collecting, moderating, analyzing, and distributing customer reviews across multiple channels. Custplace partners with major platforms including Google Business Profile, TripAdvisor, Facebook, and Pages Jaunes, offering a centralized hub for reputation management.

### ReplyStack: The Browser Extension Innovator

ReplyStack combines a browser extension with a SaaS dashboard for streamlined review response management. Rather than focusing on collection and distribution, ReplyStack specializes in helping businesses respond efficiently to reviews on any platform through AI-powered personalization.

Designed for SMBs across all industries worldwide, ReplyStack provides universal platform coverage through its extension-based approach, working on platforms with or without official APIs.

## Different Approaches to Review Management

### Custplace: Full-Cycle Review Management

Custplace addresses the entire review lifecycle:

- **Collection**: Multi-channel review solicitation (email, SMS, QR codes)
- **Moderation**: NF-certified moderation process for authenticity
- **Analysis**: AI-powered sentiment analysis and reporting
- **Distribution**: Automatic publishing to partner platforms
- **Response**: Inbox IA for centralized reply management

This comprehensive approach suits enterprises wanting a single platform for all reputation needs.

### ReplyStack: Response-Focused Efficiency

ReplyStack concentrates on one critical aspect: responding to reviews effectively.

- **Universal coverage**: Reply on any website via browser extension
- **AI personalization**: Response Profile ensures brand consistency
- **Speed**: Generate and post responses in seconds
- **Simplicity**: No complex setup or enterprise onboarding required

This focused approach serves businesses that already receive reviews and need efficient response tools.

## Key Differentiators

### Custplace Strengths

**NF Certification**
Custplace holds NF certification for its review management service, guaranteeing transparent collection processes and unbiased distribution. This certification is valuable for businesses requiring verified authenticity.

**Comprehensive Platform Partnerships**
Official partnerships with Google Business Profile, TripAdvisor, Pages Jaunes, Facebook, and Salesforce enable seamless review distribution and presence management across channels.

**Employee Review Management**
Beyond customer reviews, Custplace offers Review Jobs for managing employee feedback, providing a complete reputation solution for employer branding.

**Enterprise Analytics**
Advanced sentiment analysis, competitive intelligence, and multi-dimensional reporting (brand, store, product, employee) support data-driven decision making.

### ReplyStack Strengths

**Universal Platform Coverage**
The browser extension works on any website displaying reviews—no partnership required. Respond on Google, TripAdvisor, Booking, Yelp, Amazon, niche industry sites, and regional platforms equally.

**Direct On-Site Response**
Instead of managing responses through a separate dashboard, ReplyStack injects directly into review sites. See the review, click to generate, and post—all without leaving the platform.

**Response Profile System**
Comprehensive brand configuration ensures every response reflects your tone, values, and key differentiators consistently across all team members.

**Accessible Pricing**
Transparent pricing starting at €0/month makes professional review response accessible to any business size, from solo entrepreneurs to growing chains.

## FAQ

**Does Custplace work in English?**
Yes, Custplace offers an English interface, though its strongest presence is in French-speaking markets.

**Can I use ReplyStack for reviews collected by Custplace?**
Absolutely. ReplyStack responds to reviews regardless of their source or collection method.

**Does Custplace offer a free plan?**
Yes, Custplace offers a limited free plan with 2 sources (Google and Custplace platform).

**Which solution for restaurants?**
ReplyStack typically serves restaurants better due to its direct TripAdvisor and Google response capabilities via browser extension. Custplace suits larger restaurant chains needing collection automation.

**Is data hosted in Europe?**
Yes, both Custplace and ReplyStack host data in the European Union, ensuring GDPR compliance.
