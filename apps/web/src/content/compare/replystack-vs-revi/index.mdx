
# ReplyStack vs Revi: Complete Comparison for Review Management in 2026

Choosing the right review management solution depends on your business type and primary needs. Revi has established itself as a Spanish e-commerce focused platform, while ReplyStack offers a universal browser extension approach. This detailed comparison helps you find the best solution for your specific requirements.

## Overview of Both Solutions

### Revi: The Spanish E-commerce Specialist

Revi is a Spanish review management platform designed primarily for online shops and physical stores. Founded as a bilingual Spanish/English solution, Revi focuses on collecting verified product reviews and building customer trust through social proof.

The platform integrates with major e-commerce CMS platforms including PrestaShop, Magento, WooCommerce, Shopify, and OpenCart. Revi emphasizes review collection automation, Google Shopping ratings integration, and customizable review widgets for store pages.

### ReplyStack: The Universal Response Platform

ReplyStack combines a browser extension with a SaaS dashboard for comprehensive review response management. Rather than focusing on review collection, ReplyStack specializes in helping businesses respond efficiently to reviews on any platform through AI-powered personalization.

Designed for SMBs across all industries worldwide, ReplyStack provides universal platform coverage through its extension-based approach, working on any website displaying customer reviews.

## Different Approaches to Review Management

### Revi: Collection-Focused for E-commerce

Revi addresses the e-commerce review lifecycle:

- **Automated collection**: Post-purchase email requests for reviews
- **Product reviews**: Star ratings and detailed feedback per product
- **Store reviews**: Overall shopping experience ratings
- **Google Shopping**: Integration for product rating displays
- **Widgets**: Customizable display on product pages
- **QR codes**: Offline review collection for physical stores

This e-commerce focus makes Revi ideal for online shops wanting to build product social proof.

### ReplyStack: Response-Focused for All Platforms

ReplyStack concentrates on review response:

- **Universal coverage**: Reply on any website via browser extension
- **AI personalization**: Response Profile ensures brand consistency
- **Speed**: Generate and post responses in seconds
- **Multi-platform**: Google, TripAdvisor, Booking, Yelp, Facebook, and more
- **Dashboard**: Track responses across all locations

This response-focused approach serves businesses receiving reviews who need efficient reply tools.

## Key Differentiators

### Revi Strengths

**E-commerce Integration**
Native integration with PrestaShop, Magento, WooCommerce, Shopify, and OpenCart makes setup straightforward for online stores. Reviews automatically appear on product pages.

**Google Shopping Ratings**
Revi enables Google Shopping star ratings, increasing visibility and click-through rates for product listings in Google search results.

**Verified Purchase Reviews**
Reviews are linked to actual purchases, providing authenticity that builds customer trust. Only verified buyers can leave reviews.

**Spanish Market Focus**
As a Spanish company with bilingual support, Revi provides excellent service for Spanish and Latin American markets with native language support.

**Customizable Widgets**
Product review widgets, store badges, and public pages can be customized to match brand aesthetics.

### ReplyStack Strengths

**Universal Platform Coverage**
The browser extension works on any website displaying reviews. Respond on Google, TripAdvisor, Booking, Yelp, Amazon, niche platforms, and regional sites equally.

**AI-Powered Response Generation**
Claude-powered AI generates personalized responses in seconds, maintaining brand voice through the Response Profile system.

**Multi-Industry Support**
While Revi focuses on e-commerce, ReplyStack serves restaurants, hotels, local services, healthcare, and any business receiving reviews.

**Direct On-Site Response**
Respond directly on review platforms without leaving the site. See the review, generate response, and post—all in one workflow.

**Transparent Pricing**
Published pricing with no hidden fees allows businesses to budget accurately.

## Verdict: Which Solution to Choose?

**Revi** is the clear choice for e-commerce businesses focused on collecting product reviews and building social proof. Its integration with major shopping platforms and Google Shopping ratings provides essential tools for online stores.

**ReplyStack** is the better choice for service businesses, hospitality, and local businesses that receive reviews on multiple platforms and need efficient response tools. The AI-powered extension and universal platform coverage fill gaps that Revi doesn't address.

For pure e-commerce: Choose Revi.
For hospitality and services: Choose ReplyStack.
For hybrid businesses: Consider using both.

## FAQ

**Does Revi offer AI response generation?**
No, Revi focuses on review collection rather than AI-powered responses. ReplyStack specializes in AI response generation.

**Can ReplyStack collect reviews?**
No, ReplyStack focuses exclusively on responding to existing reviews. Use Revi or similar tools for collection.

**Does Revi work for restaurants?**
Revi is designed primarily for e-commerce and physical retail stores, not for hospitality businesses like restaurants or hotels.

**Which platform supports more languages?**
Both support multiple languages. Revi focuses on Spanish/English, while ReplyStack supports English, French, Spanish, and Portuguese.

**Can I display Revi reviews on my website?**
Yes, Revi provides customizable widgets to display product and store reviews on your e-commerce site.
