# ReplyStack vs SoLike: Complete Comparison for AI Review Responses in 2026

Both ReplyStack and SoLike offer Chrome extension-based solutions for generating AI-powered review responses. While they share similar approaches, their target markets and feature sets differ significantly. This detailed comparison helps you choose the right tool for your business needs.

## Overview of Both Solutions

### SoLike: The French Hospitality Specialist

SoLike is a French SaaS platform specializing in AI-powered review response generation. Originally focused on the hospitality industry, SoLike has expanded to serve restaurants, healthcare, automotive, and e-commerce sectors.

The platform works through a Chrome extension that analyzes review content and generates three personalized response options in the customer's language. SoLike emphasizes time savings, claiming businesses can respond to reviews 5x faster than manual methods.

### ReplyStack: The Universal Response Platform

ReplyStack combines a browser extension with a comprehensive SaaS dashboard for complete review response management. The platform focuses on brand consistency through its Response Profile system, ensuring every AI-generated response aligns with your business identity.

Designed for SMBs across all industries worldwide, ReplyStack provides multilingual support and universal platform coverage through its extension-based approach.

## Key Differentiators

### SoLike Strengths

**Auto-Post Capability**
SoLike can automatically post selected responses to certain platforms, reducing the manual step of copying and pasting. This feature saves additional time for businesses processing high volumes of reviews.

**Hospitality Focus**
Originally designed for hotels, SoLike's AI is particularly well-tuned for hospitality-specific language and scenarios. Hotels may find the responses especially relevant to their context.

**Three Response Options**
Generating three variants per review gives users more choice and flexibility in selecting the most appropriate response for each situation.

### ReplyStack Strengths

**Universal Platform Coverage**
ReplyStack's extension works on any website, not just pre-configured platforms. This means you can respond to reviews on niche industry sites, regional platforms, or any website displaying customer feedback.

**Response Profile System**
The comprehensive Response Profile captures your brand voice, values, key strengths, and signature style. This ensures consistency across all responses, regardless of who on your team generates them.

**Full SaaS Dashboard**
Beyond the extension, ReplyStack provides a complete dashboard for tracking responses, managing multiple locations, and analyzing response performance over time.

**Transparent Pricing**
Published pricing with a permanent free tier allows businesses to evaluate the product without sales conversations or commitment.

We'd be honored to welcome you back and show you the improvements. Warm regards, The Grand Hotel Team"

## Verdict: Which Solution to Choose?

**SoLike** is a solid choice for hospitality businesses, particularly hotels focused on TripAdvisor, Booking.com, and Airbnb. The auto-post feature and three response variants offer convenience for high-volume response needs. French businesses may appreciate the local support.

**ReplyStack** is the better choice for businesses needing broader platform coverage, transparent pricing, and a comprehensive management dashboard. The Response Profile system ensures brand consistency, and the permanent free plan makes it accessible for businesses of any size.

For hotels exclusively managing TripAdvisor and Booking reviews, both solutions work well. For businesses with diverse review sources including Yelp, Facebook, and industry-specific platforms, ReplyStack's universal coverage provides clear advantages.

## FAQ

**Can I use both SoLike and ReplyStack?**
Technically yes, but using both would create confusion and potentially inconsistent responses. Choose one solution for coherent brand communication.

**Does SoLike offer a free plan?**
SoLike offers a free trial but not a permanent free plan like ReplyStack.

**Which solution is faster?**
Both generate responses in seconds. SoLike's auto-post may save a few additional seconds, while ReplyStack requires copying/pasting.

**Can ReplyStack work on Booking.com?**
Yes, ReplyStack's extension works on Booking.com and all other platforms through the same universal approach.

**Are responses in my language?**
Both solutions generate responses in the customer's language automatically, supporting multilingual review management.
