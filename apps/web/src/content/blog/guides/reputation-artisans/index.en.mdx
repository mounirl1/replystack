| Element | Action | Priority |

## Getting Reviews: The Method That Works

### The contractor's challenge

Unlike a restaurant where customers can leave a review on the spot, contractors face unique challenges:

- Often short interventions (&lt; 2h)
- No "natural" moment to ask
- Customer in a hurry after the job
- Forgotten once the contractor leaves

### The solution: post-service SMS

SMS has a 98% open rate. It's your best ally.

**SMS template (to send 2-4h after the job):**

> Hi [First name], thanks for trusting me with [type of job]. If you're satisfied, a Google review would really help me out: [short link]. Thanks! - [Your name], [Company]

**Concrete example:**

> Hi Marie, thanks for trusting me with your water heater repair. If you're satisfied, a Google review would really help me out: g.page/dupont-plumbing/review. Thanks! - John, Dupont Plumbing

### The right timing

| Moment | Effectiveness | Why |
|--------|---------------|-----|
| On-site after the job | ⭐⭐ | Customer rushed, awkward |
| 2-4h after | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Customer has tested, satisfied |
| Next day | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Good for complex jobs |
| 1 week after | ⭐⭐ | Forgotten, less impact |


### What NOT to do

- ❌ Ask for a review before finishing the job
- ❌ Offer a discount for a review (prohibited)
- ❌ Explicitly ask for 5 stars
- ❌ Harass customers with texts/calls
- ❌ Create fake reviews (account suspension)

## Responding to Reviews: Contractor's Guide

![Handling difficult review situations for contractors](/blog/images/difficult-situations-reputation-artisans.webp)

### Why responding is essential

Future customers read your responses. They show:
- Your professionalism
- Your responsiveness
- Your ability to handle problems

### Response templates for contractors

**Positive review — Successful job**

> Thanks so much [First name]! Glad the work on your [equipment/issue] went well. Don't hesitate to call me back if you need anything. See you around the neighborhood!
> - John

**Positive review — Emergency handled**

> Thanks [First name]! [Problem] emergencies are never fun, but glad I could help quickly. Keep my number handy, I'm always available for my customers.
> - John

**Mixed review (4 stars)**

> Thanks for your feedback [First name]. Glad the work satisfied you. Regarding [point mentioned], I'm taking note — this kind of feedback helps me improve. Don't hesitate to call me for your next projects.
> - John

**Negative review — Legitimate problem**

> [First name], I'm sorry the job didn't meet expectations. That's not my standard of work. I'd like to come back free of charge to fix this — call me at [number]. I want you to be fully satisfied.
> - John

**Negative review — Bad faith/fake review**

> Hello, I don't have any record of a job under your name. Could you contact me at [number] with the date and address of the work? I take all complaints seriously and want to understand what happened.
> - John

### Fatal mistakes

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Do |
|---------|------|
| "You're wrong" | "I understand your frustration" |
| Ignore the review | Respond within 48h |
| Aggressive response | Stay calm and professional |
| Too many justifications | Get to the point |
| No signature | Sign with your first name |

## Handling Difficult Situations

### The legitimately unhappy customer

1. **Respond quickly** (&lt; 24h)
2. **Acknowledge** the problem
3. **Propose** a concrete solution
4. **Resolve** privately
5. **Ask** (politely) for a review update

### Fake or competitor reviews

Signs of a fake review:
- No details about the job
- Profile with no other reviews or suspicious reviews
- Date inconsistent with your jobs

**Action:**
1. Respond factually
2. Report to Google (via Google Business)
3. Document (screenshots, job records)

### The customer who asks too much

Some customers leave negative reviews for reasons beyond your control (market prices, normal job duration, etc.).

**Template response:**

> Thanks for your feedback [First name]. The rate applied is the standard rate for this type of job [brief explanation if relevant]. I'm available if you have any questions.
> - John
