## The Problem: Why Responding to Reviews Takes Too Long

### The typical process (inefficient)

Here's how most businesses handle their reviews:

```
1. Log into Google Business Profile           → 1 min
2. Read the review                            → 30 sec
3. Think about what to say                    → 2-3 min
4. Write the response                         → 3-5 min
5. Proofread and edit                         → 1 min
6. Publish                                    → 30 sec
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Total per review                              → 8-10 min
```

**For 10 reviews per week**: 1.5 hours of work.

**For 20 reviews per week**: 3 hours of work.

That's unsustainable. Result: reviews pile up without responses.

### The cost of inaction

- **Lost credibility**: Customers see you don't respond
- **Negative SEO impact**: Google favors active profiles
- **Missed opportunities**: Every review is a chance to build loyalty
- **Negative reviews that fester**: Without a response, they remain definitive

## Step 4: Handle Special Cases

### Negative reviews (priority treatment)

**Golden rule**: Always respond to negative reviews first.

**Specific workflow**:

1. **Read carefully** (10 sec) — Understand the real problem
2. **Generate AI response** (2 sec)
3. **Check the tone** (5 sec) — Empathetic? Non-defensive?
4. **Add contact info if serious** (5 sec)
5. **Publish**

**Total time**: 30-40 seconds.

**What AI does well**:
- Stay calm and professional
- Apologize without being excessive
- Offer a solution

**What you should verify**:
- Is the mentioned problem properly addressed?
- Should you offer direct contact?

### Suspicious or fake reviews

**Signs of a fake review**:
- No details about the experience
- Profile with no photo or history
- Event you can't find in your records
- Generic or inconsistent language

**Action**:
1. Respond anyway (professionally)
2. Report the review to Google
3. Don't get upset publicly

**Response template**:
> "Hello, we don't have a record of your visit in our system. Could you please contact us directly at [email] so we can identify your experience? We sincerely want to resolve any issue."

### Reviews with no text (just a rating)

Some customers leave 5 stars without a comment.

**Should you respond?** Yes, but keep it short.

**Sample response**:
> "Thanks for the 5 stars! See you soon"

**Time**: 5 seconds.

## FAQ

### "What if I have more than 10 reviews a day?"

Even with 20 reviews/day at 30 seconds each, you're at 10 minutes. Still manageable.

For very high volumes (50+ reviews/day), consider:
- Delegating to a trained employee
- Increasing your time block to 15-20 min/day

### "Can AI really personalize?"

Yes. The AI analyzes the review content (rating, text, elements mentioned) and generates an adapted response. Combined with your Response Profile, the result is personalized and consistent with your brand.

### "What if the AI gets it wrong?"

It's rare, but it can happen. Before publishing:
- Read the response (2 seconds)
- If something's off, click "Regenerate" or edit manually

You always keep final control.

### "Do I really need to respond to positive reviews?"

Yes. Customers who leave positive reviews deserve acknowledgment. Plus, other customers see you're engaged. And it's good for SEO.

### "How do I handle multiple locations?"

With ReplyStack, each location has its own Response Profile. The process stays the same: 2 minutes per location.
